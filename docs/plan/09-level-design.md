# 09 — Level Design (3 levels)

Depends on: 08. Produces: `js/10-levels.js` (data only; map strings generated by
`tools/genmaps.mjs` which is the design source of truth). Consumed by 14.
Node-testable (data only).

## Levels (name — theme — width cols — time)
1. `1-1` Sugar Meadows — day — ~112 cols — 300s. Beat: teach run/jump; bonbon
   line; Q-row with first Jelly; single Pudge patrol; first Tinshell; one pit x2;
   pipe h2 with Chomper (first scare); first trampoline over 3-wide pit; second
   Jelly; Toffee Q-block (`R`) before a Pudge pack; final bonbon arc; candy-cane
   pole + Keep. NO spikes, NO Floatoo/Hopper yet (pacing!).
2. `1-2` Candy Mine — cave — ~100 cols — 350s. Beat: low X-brick ceiling
   corridors; spikes on floor sections (`s`); Hopper debut (pairs on ledges);
   bonbon treasure alcove guarded by shells; Ember Berry (`E`) for flame play;
   pit gauntlet with trampolines; Bubble block (`O`) over a wide spike floor;
   Tinshell chain corridor (kick into partners); pole+Keep at end.
3. `1-3` Peppermint Dusk — dusk — ~118 cols — 400s. Beat: Floatoo debut over the
   opening pits (chasing balloons); Chomper pair; Hopper+Pudge mix pressure;
   trampoline chain over consecutive wide pits (the showpiece); final climb of
   X-block stairs with spike hazards, then pole+Keep. Hardest; generous bonbons.

## Design rules (apply to every level)
- Surface rows: bottom 2 rows ground (row 13-14 of 0..14). Reachable Q-rows at
  row 10 (full jump) or 11 (easy bump). High ledges 1–3 tiles thick.
- Max pit gap 4 plain, 5 with run-up, 6 only with trampoline assist.
- Every pit gets a bonbon arc overhead (reward-risk teaching).
- Difficulty curve: new mechanic introduced safe, drilled, then combined.
- Pole always on solid ground; Keep 4 cols after pole; level width = keepCol+5
  (camera clamp room). Spawn letters always on a walkable surface (Floatoo: in
  air over a pit edge). genmaps.mjs asserts the above shape rules + validator
  (17) double-checks the output maps.

## Acceptance
- Validator passes all 3; playing 1-1 demonstrates day-twists (Toffee,
  trampoline, Chomper); 1-2 demonstrates cave-twists; 1-3 demonstrates Floatoo.
